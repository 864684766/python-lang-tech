# Translate

## Rules

1. Keep proper nouns unchanged unless the glossary says otherwise.
2. First line: the translation only. Second line: `备注:` plus one short note.
3. Prefer concise wording; do not add greetings or extra commentary.

## Glossary

| Source | Target |
|---|---|
| 你好 | Hello |
| 世界 | world |
